TOURS & COMMUNITY
  • 1st Annual Baltimore Indie Bookstore Crawl
    Fri Nov 27 → Sun Nov 29 · 15 independent bookstores citywide · See site
    Baltimore's first Black Friday-weekend bookstore crawl across about fifteen indie shops — Red Emma's, Normals, Greedy Reads (Remington and Fells Point), Bird in Hand, The Ivy, Atomic Books, Charm City Books and more. Visit and buy at four or more stores, then turn in your bookstore passport for a prize drawing; extra stores mean extra entries.
    https://redemmas.org/events/1st-annual-baltimore-indie-bookstore-crawl-day-1
  • The Annual Black Friday Book Swap
    Fri Nov 27, 10 am · Red Emma's · See site
    Red Emma's yearly Black Friday swap: bring gently used books to donate, or take books home free of charge. It runs on the first day of the citywide indie bookstore crawl.
    https://redemmas.org/events/the-annual-black-friday-book-swap/
    Map: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Red%20Emma%27s%2C%203128%20Greenmount%20Ave%2C%20Baltimore%2C%20MD
  • Black Friday Music & Arts Expo
    Fri Nov 27, 2 pm · 2640 Space · Free
    Free afternoon music and arts expo at the 2640 Space on Black Friday — a DIY alternative to the malls.
    https://www.tumblr.com/baltshowplace
    Map: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=2640%20Space%2C%202640%20St%20Paul%20St%2C%20Baltimore%2C%20MD
  • Triple C Black Friday Night Market
    Fri Nov 27, 5 pm · Checkerspot Brewing Co. · Free
    Charm City Artists and Checkerspot Brewing host an evening holiday market of local artists, makers and small businesses, with DJ Mave spinning vinyl, an ugly-sweater contest and Checkerspot's beer and food.
    https://baltimore.org/event/triple-c-black-friday-night-market/
    Map: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Checkerspot%20Brewing%20Co.%2C%201421%20Ridgely%20St%2C%20Baltimore%2C%20MD%2021230
  • Krampus Hampden Yule Market
    Sat Nov 28, 10 am · Roosevelt Park, Hampden · Free
    The first Krampus Yule Market, run by the Baltimore Krampus Group and the Hampden Village Merchants Association: 30+ local vendors selling creepy holiday decor, oddities, crafts and cosplay gear, on Small Business Saturday. Holiday cosplay encouraged; bring a new unwrapped toy for Toys for Tots.
    https://baltimore.org/event/krampus-hampden-yule-market/
    Map: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Roosevelt%20Park%2C%20Hampden%2C%201221%20W%2036th%20St%2C%20Baltimore%2C%20MD
  • Bmore Zinefest
    Sun Nov 29, 4 pm · Peabody Heights Brewery · Free
    Free DIY zine fest: tables of self-published zines, comics and small-press work from Baltimore makers.
    https://www.tumblr.com/baltshowplace
    Map: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Peabody%20Heights%20Brewery%2C%20401%20E%2030th%20St%2C%20Baltimore%2C%20MD
