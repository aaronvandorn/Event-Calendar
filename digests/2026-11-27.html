1st Annual Baltimore Indie Bookstore Crawl
Fri Nov 27 → Sun Nov 29 · 15 independent bookstores citywide · See site
Baltimore's first Black Friday-weekend bookstore crawl across about fifteen indie shops — Red Emma's, Normals, Greedy Reads (Remington and Fells Point), Bird in Hand, The Ivy, Atomic Books, Charm City Books and more.
