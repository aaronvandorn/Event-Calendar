MUSEUMS & ART
  • Museo Larco – pre-Columbian collection (open daily)
    Fri Nov 27 → Fri Dec 4 · Museo Larco · S/ 50 (S/ 35 seniors 60+; S/ 25 students)
    Lima's great private museum of ancient Peruvian art, set in an 18th-century viceregal mansion in Pueblo Libre: Moche ceramics, gold and silver work, textiles, a walk-through visible storeroom of tens of thousands of pots, and the famous erotic-ceramics gallery, surrounded by bougainvillea gardens.
    https://museolarco.org/en/
    Map: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Museo%20Larco%2C%20Av.%20Sim%C3%B3n%20Bol%C3%ADvar%201515%2C%20Pueblo%20Libre%2C%20Lima
  • Contemporáneo 1. Materia :: Alquimia :: Dispositivo :: Flujo
    Fri Nov 27 → Thu Dec 31 · MAC Lima · See site
    A long-running exhibition of contemporary art from the MAC Lima collection, in Sala 3 of the Barranco museum.
    https://maclima.pe/exposiciones/
    Map: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=MAC%20Lima%2C%20Av.%20Grau%201511%2C%20Barranco%2C%20Lima%2015063
  • David Bowie: Station to Station
    Fri Nov 27 → Sat Dec 19 · Galería Earl of St Andrew's, Británico Miraflores · Free
    Exhibition tracing Bowie's reinventions from his early Shel Talmy recordings to the late 'Toy' project through photographs by Masayoshi Sukita, Mick Rock, Terry O'Neill, Brian Duffy, Andrew Kent and others, plus records and sleeves, letters, manuscripts and posters from the Perú33RPM archive. Curated by Alberto Candia.
    https://britanico.edu.pe/cultural/?post_type=bri-evt-evento&p=15109
    Map: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Galer%C3%ADa%20Earl%20of%20St%20Andrew%27s%2C%20Brit%C3%A1nico%20Miraflores%2C%20Lima%2C%20Peru
  • Donación Patricia Phelps de Cisneros. Obras para el MALI
    Fri Nov 27 → Thu Dec 31 · Museo de Arte de Lima (MALI) · See site
    Modern and contemporary works donated to MALI by collector Patricia Phelps de Cisneros, shown across Sala 2 and rooms 20 and 26 of the collection galleries.
    https://diariocorreo.pe/cultura/mali-presenta-obras-donadas-por-patricia-phelps-de-cisneros-hasta-diciembre-noticia/
    Map: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Museo%20de%20Arte%20de%20Lima%20%28MALI%29%2C%20Paseo%20Col%C3%B3n%20125%2C%20Cercado%20de%20Lima
  • El nuevo poema: obra y pensamiento de Magda Portal
    Fri Nov 27 → Sun Nov 29 · Centro Cultural Inca Garcilaso · Free
    An exhibition, curated by Kristel Best, on poet and activist Magda Portal from the 1920s to 1980 — first editions, periodicals and photographs of her avant-garde and social poetry. Housed in a colonial-era mansion in the historic centre.
    https://www.ccincagarcilaso.gob.pe/en/activities/el-nuevo-poema-obra-y-pensamiento-de-magda-portal/
    Map: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Centro%20Cultural%20Inca%20Garcilaso%2C%20Jr.%20Ucayali%20391%2C%20Cercado%20de%20Lima
  • El arte de mirarnos – 200 years of photography and dress
    Fri Nov 27 → Mon Dec 28 · Galería L'Imaginaire, Alianza Francesa de Lima · Free
    Marking 200 years of photography, curator Angela Kusen pairs archival Peruvian images with work by a new generation of Peruvian fashion photographers to look at how dressed bodies — and Peru's social and cultural changes — have been pictured across generations. French magazine Purple is a special guest.
    https://enlima.pe/agenda-cultural/exposicion/el-arte-de-mirarnos
    Map: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Galer%C3%ADa%20L%27Imaginaire%2C%20Alianza%20Francesa%20de%20Lima%2C%20Av.%20Arequipa%204595%2C%20Miraflores%2C%20Lima
  • Pacchas: agua, ritual y ofrenda en los Andes
    Fri Nov 27 → Sun Feb 28 · Museo de Arte de Lima (MALI) · See site
    Pre-Columbian and colonial ritual vessels (pacchas) shown for their ties to water, fertility and Andean practices of offering and reciprocity.
    https://www.arteinformado.com/agenda/f/pacchas-agua-ritual-y-ofrenda-en-los-andes-247196
    Map: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Museo%20de%20Arte%20de%20Lima%20%28MALI%29%2C%20Paseo%20Col%C3%B3n%20125%2C%20Cercado%20de%20Lima
  • El tejido como memoria. Centro de Textiles Tradicionales del Cusco
    Fri Nov 27 → Sun Mar 14 · Museo de Arte de Lima (MALI) · See site
    Work by Cusco weavers carrying on textile techniques passed down through generations, presented as living Andean heritage.
    https://www.arteinformado.com/agenda/f/el-tejido-como-memoria-centro-de-textiles-tradicionales-del-cusco-247197

  • Playground: Fernando Dolorier
    Fri Nov 27, 10 am → Thu Dec 10 · Museo Metropolitano de Lima · Free

  • Adam's Rib (George Cukor, 1949) – XII Ciclo La Música en el Cine
    Sat Nov 28, 11 am · Cine del Centro Cultural PUCP · Free
    Saturday-morning screening of Cukor's 1949 black-and-white comedy in which two married New York lawyers end up on opposite sides of a courtroom case about a woman who shot her unfaithful husband. Part of the Sociedad Filarmónica de Lima / Filmoteca PUCP cycle on music in film.
